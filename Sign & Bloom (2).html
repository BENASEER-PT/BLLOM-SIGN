Sign Lab

See it. Sign it. Keep it.

Each card shows where on your body the sign happens, how your hands move and what your face should do. Watch the pink dot to see which way your hand moves, open a video, and mark signs you know to earn petals.

These cards follow American Sign Language as a starter set. Indian Sign Language and other sign languages use different signs for many words, so check with a deaf teacher or the ISLRTC before you rely on a sign.

Watch signs in real life

Type any word and open video results in a new tab. Choose clips from deaf signers, ideally with captions. The pink dot on each card is only a guide to hand movement, so use a video to check handshapes.
